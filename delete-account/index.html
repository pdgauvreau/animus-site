Delete your account

Updated October 12, 2026

You can delete your Animus account and the data that goes with it at any time, either in the app or by email. Deleting is permanent and can't be undone.

In the app

This works right away.

  1. Open Animus and sign in.
  2. Tap your profile picture at the top of the screen.
  3. Tap Settings.
  4. Scroll to the Account section.
  5. Press and hold Hold to delete account until it fills. Your account is deleted and you are signed out.

By email

If you can't open the app, or no longer have it installed, email us from the address you use to sign in to Animus. Put "Delete my account" in the subject. We may reply to confirm the request comes from you, and we will delete your account within 30 days.

Email animuscomp@gmail.com

You can also email us to ask for specific posts, photos or videos to be removed without deleting your whole account.

What gets deleted

  • Your account: your email address and sign-in details
  • Your profile: name, color, profile photo, and the pillars you chose
  • Your play history: cards, completions, streaks, swaps, points, Draft ballots and votes
  • Everything you posted: proof photos and videos, captions, puzzle results, comments and reactions
  • Your settings, reminder time, and push token
  • Your place in every league. If you were a league's commissioner, the longest-standing member takes over. If you were the last member, the league is deleted too.

When it's deleted

  • In the app: immediately.
  • By email: within 30 days of your request.
  • Backups: any copies in our backups are overwritten within 30 days.

We don't keep any of your data after that, unless the law requires us to keep something specific. Raw Health Connect readings never leave your phone. The only health-related data we hold is the results you posted (like "12.4K steps"), and those are deleted with your posts. You can remove Animus's access to Health Connect in your phone's settings.

More about how we handle your data is in our privacy policy.
