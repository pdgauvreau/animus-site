How it works

Showing up beats talent.

Seasons are short, the rules are simple, and the scoreboard is honest.

  1. Form a league

    Invite your friends to a private league. Seasons run 2 or 4 weeks, so there is always a finish line in sight.

  2. Draw your rituals

    Every day brings ritual cards across four pillars. You do them in real life, not in the app.

  3. Post the proof

    Snap a photo or a short video so your league knows it happened. Proof is shared with your league only.

  4. Climb the standings

    Every ritual earns points. When the season ends, one name wears the laurel.

The four pillars

Four ways to get better.

Every ritual belongs to a pillar. Balance them, or lean into the one you need most.

Mind

Read, learn, focus. Sharpen the thing that runs everything else.

Body

Move, sweat, stretch. Show up for the body you live in.

Light

Sunlight, sleep, stillness. The quiet habits that keep you steady.

Tribe

Call, help, gather. The people around you are part of the work.
