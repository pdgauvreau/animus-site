The short version

Animus is a game you play with friends. What you share goes to the people in your leagues, not to the public, advertisers, or data brokers. We don't sell your data, there are no ads, and we don't track you across other apps or websites.

What we collect

Account

Your email address and a password. The password is stored by our sign-in provider as a secure hash, never in plain text.

Profile

Your name, the color or profile photo you choose, and the pillars you told us you want more of.

Play

Your ritual cards, which cards you complete and when, streaks, swaps, Draft ballots, votes on challenges, points and standings, and whether league stakes were marked as settled.

Photos and videos you post as proof

Proof photos and selfie videos you take in the app (or a screen recording you choose to attach), along with captions, puzzle results you paste, comments, reactions, and reports you make. The app uses your camera to take proof, and your microphone to record sound with selfie videos. It only uses them while you are capturing proof.

Location

When you take a proof photo or video and the "Add the place" setting is on, the app asks your phone for its location once, using precise or approximate location depending on what you allow. Your phone turns those coordinates into a short place name, like "Riverside Park, Denver." On Android, that lookup is done by your phone's built-in geocoding service, which is provided by Google. We store the place name on the stamp, not your coordinates. The app does not track your location in the background. Turn the setting off and only the time and date are stamped.

Health Connect (optional)

If you turn it on, Animus reads your step count, your exercise sessions (walks and runs), and their distance from Health Connect, only with your permission and only to complete ritual cards like 10K Steps. Animus never writes to your health data. The raw readings stay on your phone; only the result (like "12.4K steps") is posted to your league. Health data is never used for advertising, never sold, and never shared except to show that result to your league.

Settings and device

Your reminder time, notification choices, time zone, and, if you allow notifications, a push token. A push token is an identifier for your app install that lets us send you notifications. It is not your phone number or contacts.

How we use it

  • To run the game: build your daily cards, score your rituals, and keep your league's standings.
  • To show your posts, proof, comments and reactions to the members of your leagues.
  • To send the reminders and league notifications you turned on.
  • To keep Animus safe: review reports, remove content that breaks our rules, and stop abuse.
  • To answer you when you contact us.

We don't use your information for advertising or to build profiles about you.

Who sees it

  • Your leagues. League members see your name, profile, standings, posts, comments and reactions. Photos and videos are stored privately and shown only to members of the league you posted them in.
  • Nobody sees Draft ballots. Only the totals are shown.
  • Our service providers, listed below, process data only to run Animus for us.
  • The law. We may share information if the law requires it, or to protect someone's safety.

Service providers

  • Supabase is our main processor. It hosts our database, sign-in, and file storage, so it holds your account, profile, play data, posts, photos and videos on our behalf.
  • Expo delivers app updates and routes push notifications.
  • Google (Firebase Cloud Messaging) delivers push notifications to Android phones. It receives your push token and the notification text.
  • Google (Android geocoding) turns your location into a place name when "Add the place" is on, as described above.

These providers may store data on servers outside your country. They are only allowed to use it to provide their service to us.

What we don't do

No ads. No selling or renting your data. No tracking you across other apps or websites. No access to your contacts, and no access to your photo library beyond what you choose to attach.

How long we keep it

  • We keep your information while your account is open, so your leagues and history work.
  • When you delete your account in the app, it is deleted right away.
  • If you ask us by email, we delete your account within 30 days.
  • Copies in our backups are overwritten as backups age out, within 30 days.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time. In the app, tap your profile picture, open Settings, and press and hold Hold to delete account in the Account section. Or email animuscomp@gmail.com from the address on your account. Deleting removes your profile, posts, comments, results, photos, videos, push token, and your place in every league. Full details are on the delete account page.

Your choices and rights

  • Turn off "Add the place", notifications, and Health Connect access at any time, in the app or in your phone's settings.
  • Edit your name and profile photo in the app.
  • Block someone to stop seeing their posts and comments, and report anything that shouldn't be there.
  • Ask us for a copy of your information, to correct it, or to delete it, by emailing animuscomp@gmail.com. Depending on where you live, you may have other rights under local law, and we will honor them.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit. Photos and videos are kept in private storage that only members of the right league can open, and access rules in our database limit each person to what they're allowed to see. No system is perfect, but we work to protect your information and will tell you if a breach affects you.

Children

Animus is not meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information. If you think a child under 13 has an account, email us and we will delete it.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we will tell you in the app and update the date at the top of this page.
